# The cleanjibe.org homepage, rebuilt (28 September 2026)

**Status:** built on branch `fb/homepage`, not pushed; Jan reviews first.
**Surfaces:** `web/index.html`, `web/css/front.css`, the header of the five reader pages, and
the app's *What CleanJibe does*, which now shares one basis with the homepage.

Jan: *"Pls put significant effort in it. It's really important."* The homepage is the one
page of the site that is **not a copy of the app**. It is written for a wingfoiler who has
none of our apps yet, and most of them arrive on a phone at the beach. Its job is to make
that rider want it within ten seconds, and then send him to the way in that fits his kit.

## 1. What was wrong with the old page

The UX review file the brief names (`scratchpad/ux-review/family.md`) was not on disk, so
this list comes from reading the page as a rider.

- **Two equal doors and a third ghost.** *Get the beta*, *Open the app* and *See a real
  session*. The fastest wow, the example, was the quietest button.
- **"The three pieces" was the product's anatomy, not the rider's question.** A rider asks
  *will it work with my watch?*, and the answer was spread over three cards and a link.
- **Nothing about the sessions he already has.** Most riders own years of sessions on Garmin
  Connect. The old page never said they can come in.
- **The hero card was the old layout,** nine tiles, from before layout B v2.
- **The header** carried Get started, Help and a pill with an underlined label in it.

## 2. The structure, top to bottom

| # | band | what the rider learns | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hero** | the question, the promise, the three verdicts, one big button into the example, the real share card beside it | kit: `hero` (web), `legend` and `example` (both) |
| 2 | **What you get** | the four things measured, then what each verdict means with the clean star | kit: `measures` (both), `verdicts` (web) |
| 3 | **Which way in is yours?** | four cards by kit: Garmin and iPhone, Garmin without iPhone, Apple Watch, another watch or Strava. Each card prints the family's honest line for every app it names, and buttons that name their app | kit: `family` (both), `chooser` (web) |
| 4 | **Bring in the sessions you already have** | any Garmin profile counts, intervals.icu brings two years, one file works too | kit: `oldSessions` (web) |
| 5 | **Get much more with the watch app** | verdicts on the wrist, pump strokes and takeoffs, live numbers, the watch count | kit: `watchApp` (web) |
| 6 | **We ride too** | no account, no server, open source, we read every mail; the community sentence; mail and GitHub | kit: `trust` (web), `community` (both) |

**Reasoning.**

- **The question is the headline** because the rider arrives with it. The promise is
  pinned copy (`docs/copy/phrases.json`), so it is split at its question rather than
  rewritten; one wrapper keeps the pin reading the whole sentence.
- **The artefact is real.** The card is the example session drawn by `js/sharecard.js`
  (layout B v2, map on), not a mock-up and not a stock photo. It links to the example.
- **One loud button in the hero.** *Try the example session* opens the example in the
  browser app with nothing to install and nothing to sign up for. The second button jumps
  to the chooser. Both are full width on a phone, inside the first screen.
- **The chooser is honest about each way.** The browser app is "most of the same
  analysis"; the Apple Watch card carries the Beta pill and says the watch app comes with
  the iPhone app. Buttons say what they do: *Get the iPhone app*, *Put it on your watch*,
  *Try it in your browser*.
- **Old sessions before the watch app**, as the brief orders: first what he gets for free
  from what he already has, then what more the watch app adds.
- **Trust last**, where a reader who is nearly convinced looks for the catch.

## 3. One basis for both front doors

Jan's decision of the same day: the homepage and the app's *What CleanJibe does* are drawn
from **one ordered list**, `WelcomeSections` in the kit
(`ios/WingFoilKit/Sources/WingFoilKit/Help/WelcomeSections.swift`). Each section carries its
words and `surfaces: app | web | both`.

- The kit exports it to `docs/copy/welcome.json`
  (`COPY_WRITE=1 swift test --filter WelcomeSectionsExportTests`), and that test fails when
  the JSON is stale.
- `web/tools/make_home.py` renders `<main>` of `web/index.html` from the export, and
  `docs/web-parity/welcome-vs-homepage.md`, the list of differences, from `surfaces`.
- `WelcomeView.swift` draws `WelcomeSections.app` in order through one `switch`;
  `WelcomeGuide`'s old constants now read the sections, so no wording moved.
- `make_home.py --check`, run by `verify_links.py`, fails when the page or the parity doc is
  stale, when WelcomeView lacks a `case` for an app section, and when WelcomeView types a
  word a shared section owns.

**Shared (both):** `legend`, `example`, `measures`, `family`, `community`.
**Homepage only:** `hero`, `verdicts`, `chooser`, `oldSessions`, `watchApp`, `trust`.
**App only:** `identity`, `getStarted`, `footerLinks`.

One visible consequence: the hero's first button reads *Try the example session*, the app's
own words, rather than the brief's *See a real session*. One door, one name.

**What is the web's own and not in the kit:** the links (a section names an action by id;
`ACTIONS` in `make_home.py` maps ids to addresses), the card's alt text, the watch count
(`make_devices.py` keeps it), the `<head>`, the header and the footer.

## 4. The copy

Every sentence is in `WelcomeSections.swift`, which the voice check reads as kit copy. The
new sentences, by band:

- Hero note: *It is free and needs no account. The example opens right here in your
  browser.*
- Chooser cards: *You get everything. Add the watch app for a verdict on your wrist after
  every jibe.* · *The browser app works on Android and on any computer. Your sessions stay
  in that browser.* · *The watch app comes with the iPhone app. Apple's own Workout app
  works too, through Apple Health.* · *Strava comes in on the iPhone. A .fit file works in
  both apps.* Under them: *The iPhone app installs through Apple's TestFlight app. It is
  free, and there is no invite to wait for.*
- Old sessions: *Your last season is already on your watch account. Connect it once and
  CleanJibe reads every session you rode.* Then *Any Garmin profile*, *Through
  intervals.icu*, *Or one file at a time*.
- Watch app: *The free CleanJibe app for Garmin records a wingfoil session the way we read
  one.* Then *Verdicts on your wrist*, *Pump strokes and takeoffs*, *Live on the water*,
  and *It runs on 42 Garmin watches, from the fenix 8 down to the vívoactive.*
- We ride too: *No account*, *No server*, *Open source*, *We read every mail*, then the
  kit's community sentence.

## 5. The header, on every reader page

Jan, on a photo of the old header: the wordmark, which links home, and **one button**,
*Try it in your browser*, the chooser's own words for the browser. Get started and Help
left the header; they are in the footer of every page and in the app's menu. `a.btn`
carries no underline any more (`css/style.css`), which was the underlined label inside
the filled pill. The block is still copied verbatim on the five pages and held identical
by `verify_links.py`.

## 6. Fast, accessible, findable

- **No Pyodide, no script** on the page beyond the topbar's eight lines and umami.
- **The card** is 29 KB at 1x and 65 KB at 2x, preloaded, with its box reserved by
  `aspect-ratio`. Its alt text names every number on it.
- **Both themes** through the site's tokens; the verdict inks are the design tokens.
- **Contrast** is the site's own ink scale; body text on the bands is `--ink-2`.
- **SEO:** a title with the question in it, a description, canonical, `en-GB`, JSON-LD
  `SoftwareApplication`, and an Open Graph image that **is the card**: the example's
  landscape card on a 1200x630 plate (`web/img/og-card.png`). The other pages keep
  `social-card.png`.

## 7. The rider-perspective pass

A wingfoiler at the van, phone in a wet hand:

- **Second 1–3.** *Did you fly through that jibe?* He did or he did not, and he wants to
  know. Three coloured chips say what the answer looks like.
- **Second 4–7.** The big blue button says *Try the example session*, and the card under
  it shows a real afternoon on Garda with five clean jibes. The line under the button says
  it is free, needs no account and opens right here.
- **Second 8–10.** He taps. Or he taps *Which way in is yours?* and finds *A Garmin and an
  iPhone* first.

Things this pass changed: the chooser buttons fill their card; the term lists became one
panel with rows, so eight definitions are a screen and a half on a phone and not three; the
section buttons outside the chooser are quiet, so the page has one loud button per band.

Things it did not settle, for Jan:

1. **The App Store link.** The site links the iPhone app through TestFlight only, because
   no App Store address exists anywhere in the repo. When the release is live, *Get the
   iPhone app* should go to the store and the beta should be a second, smaller line.
2. **The brief's *See a real session*** became the app's *Try the example session*, by the
   shared-basis rule. Say if the homepage should win and the app change with it.
3. **Word budget.** The page is about 840 words against a budget raised from 550 to 900.
4. **The family's title**, *The CleanJibe family*, is not printed on the homepage: the
   chooser's question is the heading, and the family's intro is its lede.
