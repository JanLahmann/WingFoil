What’s new

The release notes

Newest first. The iPhone app ships as TestFlight builds. The watch app ships as Connect IQ versions. The two number differently and land on different days.

TestFlight offers the newest build by itself. Garmin Connect updates the watch app. A report is worth more with a build number in it. You find yours at the foot of the app’s menu, and under Settings → About.
