What’s new

Newest first. The iPhone app ships as TestFlight builds and the watch app as Connect IQ versions, so the two number differently and land on different days.

Not installed yet? Both installs are here, and the start guide is what to do with them once they are on — one session on the water, read against what you remember.

Releases

TestFlight offers the newest build automatically; the Connect IQ store updates the watch app through Garmin Connect. A build number is what a bug report should name — it is at the foot of the app’s menu, and under Settings → About.

iPhone · build 49

13 September 2026 · 0.15.0

A crash fix for the watch link.

  • The app no longer crashes when the Garmin sends it something — a summary card, or an answer to a map push. Builds 43 to 47 fell over the moment a message arrived from a watch running a CleanJibe beta build.
  • Worth checking: send a map to the watch, finish a short recording on the watch, and see whether the phone stays up and the session appears.

iPhone · build 47

13 September 2026 · 0.15.0

You choose which maps the watch gets.

  • Map for the watch is a page now: the two most-ridden spots by default, or up to two picks among your own spots and Where I am now. Settings → Garmin watch → Map for the watch.
  • The phone reads its own position once per send, only when you picked it, and never in the background. The tick asks for the permission where you can see it; the automatic send at launch never prompts.

iPhone · build 45

13 September 2026 · 0.15.0

The session list learns to group and to narrow — and every page gets a way to complain.

  • Group by None, Month, Year or Spot at the top of Sessions, newest group first. A library of twenty or more opens grouped by month until you say otherwise.
  • A filter beside Import: one spot, one source (intervals.icu, Strava, Apple Health, the Garmin watch, a file), a date window. What is active shows as chips you can tap away, and the count line reads 3 of 41 sessions. Records and Trends keep reading the whole library on purpose.
  • The menu at the top left of Sessions, in the order a new rider needs it: Getting started, Settings, Support, then What CleanJibe does and What the numbers mean, with the build number at the foot. The welcome screen has a close button now.
  • “Something off? Send feedback” at the foot of every page — and on a session page the mail names the session by itself.
  • Connect Strava from Settings → Strava as well as from Import.
  • The full-screen map’s legend no longer floats to the middle of the screen after a keyboard was used somewhere else.

iPhone · build 43

13 September 2026 · 0.15.0

Two fixes from the first day of build 41.

  • Send map to watch now finds whichever CleanJibe build you have on the watch — release, public beta or dev — instead of assuming the release one. “Failure_AppNotFound” is gone.
  • The turn page scrolls from anywhere again: a vertical finger on a strip scrolls the page, a sideways one scrubs the playhead, a tap places it. The small window words no longer overprint each other.

iPhone · build 41

13 September 2026 · 0.15.0

The session video, and a much wider way in.

  • Export video: a session becomes a short film — the map draws itself along your track, the numbers land, and it shares like any other clip. Open a session → share → Export video.
  • Send map to watch: the phone draws the shore of your spot and pushes it to a Garmin running CleanJibe 0.9.10, so the live map has ground under the breadcrumb. Settings → Garmin watch.
  • Imports take GPX and TCX as well as FIT — so a Polar, Suunto or COROS session comes in through intervals.icu or straight from the vendor’s app through the share sheet. Without a speed channel the speed records are estimated and marked uncertified.
  • Import from Strava: connect your account under Import and pick the sessions. Positions only, so speed records are uncertified — and single-rider until Strava approves the app.
  • Analysis engine 0.18.0: the pump rung now asks the minimum foil speed, and every touchdown and fall says why it ended that way on the turn page.

Garmin watch app · 0.9.10

13 September 2026

Nine more watches, and the shore under the trail.

  • Nine more watches: Venu 2 / 2S / 2 Plus / 3 / 3S, vívoactive 5 / 6 and Instinct 3 AMOLED 45 / 50 mm — 39 products in all. They pass the layout checks in the simulator and have not been ridden on a real watch; if you have one, tell us how the pages look and whether the touch controls behave.
  • The live map page and the post-save Track page can draw the shore of your spot under the trail, sent over Bluetooth from the CleanJibe iPhone app (Settings → Garmin watch → Send map to watch). No firmware map view is involved, which is the whole point — the firmware’s own map kills the app on a fenix 8.
  • A splash page with the mark on the first launch after an install or update, and a larger mark on the start page.

0.9.10 is uploaded and the public store listing still shows 0.9.9 while it goes through Garmin’s review. Which watch has the full product list.

Garmin watch app · 0.9.9

9 September 2026

A clean jibe now needs a quiet ten seconds after the turn.

  • No touchdown, no swim and no dunked wrist in the ten seconds after the sweep — so the star, the clean count and CPH on the wrist mean what the phone and cleanjibe.org mean by them.
  • The clean-jibe buzz therefore arrives up to ten seconds after the turn. The flew through, touched down and fell in verdicts are unchanged and still buzz at once.

Older watch-app versions — 0.9.8 back to 0.9.0 — are in the Connect IQ listing’s own What’s New, and the whole history of both apps, commit by commit, is in the public repository.
