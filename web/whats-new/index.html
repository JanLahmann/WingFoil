What’s new

Newest first. The iPhone app ships as TestFlight builds. The watch app ships as Connect IQ versions. The two number differently and land on different days.

Not installed yet? Both installs are here.

Watch app today: 0.9.13, on 42 Connect IQ products. The list runs family by family. The cards below say what each release said on the day it shipped.

Releases

TestFlight offers the newest build automatically. The Connect IQ store updates the watch app through Garmin Connect. A bug report should name a build number. You find it at the foot of the app’s menu, and under Settings → About.

Garmin watch app · 0.9.9

9 September 2026

A clean jibe needs ten quiet seconds.

  • No touchdown, no swim and no dunked wrist in the ten seconds after the sweep.
  • The star, the clean count and CPH on the wrist now mean what the phone means.
  • cleanjibe.org means the same by them.
  • The clean-jibe buzz therefore arrives up to ten seconds after the turn.
  • The flew through, touched down and fell in verdicts are unchanged.
  • They still buzz at once.
