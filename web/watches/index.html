Which watch

The CleanJibe watch app is a Connect IQ app, so it runs on the Garmins that Garmin lets it run on — 42 products as of 0.9.11, listed in full below.

What you need, what you get

Class You record with What you get
Class A · Garmin watch app The CleanJibe watch app on a Garmin Everything: foil time, flights, every turn verdict and clean jibe, certified speed records, the wind axis, pump strokes and takeoff attempts.
Class B · any speed-certified file Any other FIT with a measured speed channel — Garmin’s own Windsurf profile, another Connect IQ app Everything except pump strokes and takeoff attempts, which need a wrist accelerometer nothing else records. Speed records certify.
Class B+ · Apple Watch appbeta The CleanJibe Apple Watch app: class B speed, plus the 50 Hz wrist accelerometer Everything class B gets, and pump strokes and takeoff attempts too: the watch app records the wrist at 50 Hz and the phone analyses it.
Class C · positions only Positions only: Strava, a .gpx, a .tcx without speed, a phone in a pocket Foil time, flights, every turn verdict and clean jibe, the wind axis. Speed records are estimated from positions and marked uncertified, and there are no pump strokes or takeoff attempts.

beta in the public beta today, not yet in the App Store release.

Not on the list, and why

The app needs Connect IQ 3.3.3 and about 770 KB of app memory. The Instinct 2 family and Instinct 3 Solar have 96–128 KB and cannot run it at all; neither can the plain fenix 5 and 5S (128 KB), the fenix 5X (Connect IQ 3.x without System 5), or the fenix 6 bases. The fenix 5 Plus, 5S Plus and 5X Plus are a different watch and run it from 0.9.11 on — they are in the table above. The rectangular Venu X1 is waiting for a rectangular layout rather than for a decision.

Every other watch

The analysis reads a file, not a brand. Drop a .fit, .gpx or .tcx from anything into the browser analyzer, or share it into the iPhone app.

Take the FIT wherever an app offers you one: it carries the speed your watch measured, which is the whole difference between class B and class C.

Apple Watch

Two routes, and the second one is ours. Both need the iPhone app; neither needs a Garmin anywhere.

Apple's own Workout appbeta

Record a Surfing or Water Sports workout the way you already do, then in CleanJibe use Import → Apple Health. It reads the route and the heart rate, nothing else, and the speed is the watch's own, so records from it certify. Switch on the automatic pickup and later workouts arrive by themselves.

The CleanJibe watch appbeta

CleanJibe’s own Apple Watch app comes with the TestFlight install — there is nothing else to download. It records straight to the phone: no Health round trip, no file to move, and the speed is the watch’s own, so its records certify. It also records the 50 Hz wrist accelerometer, which is what makes it the one Apple Watch route with pump strokes and takeoff attempts (b + wrist).

No watch at all

A phone in a waterproof pouch records a .gpx, and a .gpx is enough for most of what this thing says: the track, the flights, the jibes and their verdicts, the wind axis. That is class c, and nothing else is missing.

iPhone

Apple’s own Fitness and Workout apps need an Apple Watch, so on a phone alone they are not the answer. Three that are:

  • Strava — the simplest, because no file has to move. Record with Record in the Strava app, then in CleanJibe use Import → Strava and pick the session.
  • Open GPX Tracker — free, open source, no account. Record, then share the .gpx and choose Open in CleanJibe, or drop it into the analyzer.
  • Komoot — record a tour, then Download GPX file. The region you start in has to be unlocked; one is free.

Android

There is no CleanJibe app for Android and none is needed: open cleanjibe.org/app in the phone browser and drop the file in.

Install it, and it joins the share sheet. In Chrome, take Add to Home screen (or Install app) from the menu. It gets an icon, opens without browser chrome and works with no signal. After that, hold a .fit or .gpx anywhere on the phone and choose Share → CleanJibe: the analysis opens on it, with the file still on the phone.

  • Strava — record in the app; the export is on strava.com, so open the activity there and take Export GPX.
  • OsmAnd — Trip recording, then My Places → Tracks → Share.
  • GPSLogger — a plain, tiny logger that writes GPX and shares it. On F-Droid, not the Play Store.
  • Komoot — as on the iPhone; the GPX lands in Downloads.

However you record it

  • Phone in a waterproof pouch on your body — on the board it is a swim away from being gone, and in a bag on the beach it records the beach.
  • Start it before you launch, stop it once you are ashore. The analysis finds the riding inside the standing about at both ends.
  • Then export the file and share it into CleanJibe — or connect Strava and skip the file altogether.
  • A phone on your body is a noisier receiver than a watch, so treat the top speed as a story rather than a record.
