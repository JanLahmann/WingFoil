Beta test · one session on the water

Get started with the CleanJibe beta

The test is one ordinary session. Ride the way you always do, then open it on the phone and read the verdicts against what you remember — that jibe you touched down in, the one you flew through and knew it. Nobody but you can check that, which is the whole reason this beta exists.

The walkthrough is below: charge, record, save, sync, read, share a card, tell us. Then the route for your own watch, what to say about it and where to put it.

No wind this week? There is a 20-minute check on land further down — it proves the recording reaches the phone, which is the half of the route that goes wrong.

Before you start

Four things, and the last one is the one people skip.

  1. Install the iPhone app

    The Garmin, the file and the Strava routes work in every build of CleanJibe. The two Apple Watch routes are beta doors, and the beta is one tap away: on the iPhone open testflight.apple.com/join/nygqGGcn, install Apple's TestFlight app if it asks, then install CleanJibe through it. Your library comes with you either way.

    Open CleanJibe once so it can ask for its permissions, and allow Health — that is the only one the beta asks for, and the app never asks for your position at all.

    If TestFlight offers an update for CleanJibe, take it before you start. It is worth being on the newest build for a test whose whole point is telling us what broke.

  2. Garmin riders: install the watch app

    CleanJibe Wingfoil Tracker (Beta) on the Connect IQ store — from the web store, or from Connect IQ inside the Garmin Connect app on your phone. It is an open beta: no key, no account, nothing to request.

  3. Watch on the wrist, unlocked, paired

    Both watch routes want the watch you actually wear, paired with the iPhone you installed CleanJibe on.

  4. Go outside

    Every route needs GPS, and a watch indoors records no positions at all — which produces exactly the empty session that looks like a bug and is not one.

On the water — the real test

One normal session. The only extra work is the last two steps, and they are the ones this beta cannot do without.

  1. Charge the watch, and the phone

    A GPS activity with the screen on is the most expensive thing a watch does, and a session that stops at 70 % battery is a session nobody can read a verdict out of. Top both up the evening before.

  2. Start CleanJibe on the watch, on the beach

    Press START, pick CleanJibe from the activity list, and wait until it says GPS good before you press START again. Do it while you are rigging: a fix takes longer than you think and the first minutes of a session are the ones you are least willing to spend standing still.

  3. Ride, and glance at the wrist once

    Ride the session you came for. Somewhere in the middle, look at the tally — flew, touched down, fell in — and the dry streak, and see whether they match the last ten minutes. That one glance is worth more than a paragraph written afterwards.

  4. Stop and save

    STOP, then Save. Flick through the summary pages once (UP/DOWN) before you put the watch away and note anything that looks wrong.

  5. Let Garmin Connect sync

    The watch uploads to Garmin Connect by itself next to the phone — open Garmin Connect once if it is taking its time. From there intervals.icu brings it to CleanJibe; that bridge is set up once and never again.

    Apple Watch, Polar, Suunto, COROS or a phone: your recording gets in a different way, and the routes are the next section down. Everything after this step is the same.

  6. Pull down on Sessions

    Open CleanJibe on the iPhone and pull down on the Sessions list. That is the refresh: it checks intervals.icu, imports what is new, and analyses it as it lands. Your session appears at the top with the day’s numbers on it.

  7. Open it, and check the verdicts against your memory

    This is the test. Open the session, go to Turns, and read down the list: every turn has a verdict — flew through, touched down, fell in — and the clean jibes are marked among them. Tap one and the page says why it was scored that way.

    Where does it disagree with you? A jibe you know you flew through that is marked as a touchdown, a swim it never noticed, a wind direction that is not the one you sailed in. Those three sentences are the most useful report this project can get.

  8. Share a card

    Share button, top right → share card: your track, your numbers, one picture. Send it to whoever you were on the water with. It is also how anyone else finds out this exists, so tell us if anything on it reads wrong or looks cheap.

  9. Send the report: Menu → Support

    Top left on the Sessions tab: Menu → Support. The build, your phone, your iOS version, your watch and the size of your library are already typed in; you add what the session got right and what it got wrong, and press Send. The other doors are at the bottom of this page.

The routes — one per watch

The real test is one session on the water: record it the way you always do, bring it in, and read the turn verdicts against what you remember — which jibes you flew through, where you touched down, where you fell in.

Take the one that matches what is on your wrist. Every route ends the same way: the session lands in CleanJibe, analysed as it arrives.

beta in the public beta today, not yet in the App Store release.

Route A · Garmin with the CleanJibe watch app

fenix, epix, Forerunner and the rest

Install it from Connect IQ, ride, save. Sessions sync through intervals.icu: Settings → intervals.icu, four steps, once.

  1. Install the watch app

    CleanJibe Wingfoil Watch App on the Connect IQ store, from the web or from Connect IQ inside the Garmin Connect app. No key, no account.

  2. Record and save

    Press START, pick CleanJibe from the activity list, wait until it says GPS good, press START again. Ride, then STOP and Save.

  3. Let Garmin Connect sync

    The watch uploads to Garmin Connect by itself next to the phone. Open Garmin Connect once if it is taking its time.

  4. Bridge it with intervals.icu

    Free account at intervals.icu, connect Garmin there, copy your personal API key, paste it into Settings → intervals.icu. Four steps, about five minutes, once.

  5. Pull down on Sessions

    That is the refresh: CleanJibe checks intervals.icu, imports what is new and analyses it as it lands. Your session appears at the top.

  6. No intervals.icu? The long way

    connect.garmin.com → the activity → the gear icon → Export File. AirDrop it to the iPhone, then Import → FIT or ZIP….

Route B · Any watch that writes a .fit

Polar, Suunto, COROS and everything else

Sync it through intervals.icu, or share the file into CleanJibe from Files, Mail, AirDrop or any share sheet.

  1. Record on your own watch

    Any outdoor activity with GPS, recorded the way you always do. There is nothing of ours to install on these watches.

  2. Export the workout as a file

    Suunto: Calendar → the workout → ⋯ → FIT. COROS: Activities → the activity → ⋯ → Export → FIT. Polar exports from flow.polar.com only.

  3. Take the FIT where you are offered one

    A FIT carries the speed your watch measured, a .gpx does not, and a .tcx only when the app wrote it in.

  4. Hand it to CleanJibe

    Open in CleanJibe from the iOS share sheet, or Import → FIT or ZIP…. Foil time, flights, turns and the map are identical either way.

  5. Or let intervals.icu do it

    Most of these watches sync to intervals.icu too, and CleanJibe picks them up from there — the same four steps as the Garmin route.

Route C · Strava

any watch that syncs to Strava

Settings → Strava → Connect with Strava, then Import → Import from Strava…. Positions only, so speed records are uncertified.

  1. Connect once

    Settings → Strava → Connect with Strava. Strava’s own consent page opens; leave the private-activities box ticked or your “Only you” sessions stay hidden.

  2. Pick your sessions

    Import → Import from Strava… lists your matching activities of the last two years. Tap the ones you want, or Import all new.

  3. Keep it automatic

    Once one session has come in this way a toggle appears, and CleanJibe checks Strava whenever you open the app.

  4. What it costs

    Strava hands over positions rather than the speed your watch measured, so these speed records are marked uncertified, and there are no pump strokes.

  5. If connecting is refused

    Strava lets a new app connect a limited number of riders; that is nothing to do with your account. Menu → Support & ideas says so.

Route D · The CleanJibe Apple Watch appbeta

Apple Watch · everything a Garmin gets

Record on your Apple Watch and the session comes to the phone by itself — pump strokes and takeoff attempts included.

  1. Put it on the watch

    It comes with the CleanJibe beta. On the iPhone open Apple’s Watch app, scroll to Available apps, and install CleanJibe if it is missing.

  2. Record

    Open CleanJibe on the watch, allow Health and Location if it asks, wait until NO GPS clears, press START. Then Stop, and Done.

  3. Let it arrive

    Open CleanJibe on the iPhone. The session comes across on its own while phone and watch are together; give it a minute.

  4. What you get

    Speed off the watch’s own receiver, so the records certify, and the 50 Hz wrist, so pump strokes and takeoff attempts are analysed.

Route E · Apple’s own Workout appbeta

Apple Watch · nothing to install

No extra app on the watch: record a Surfing workout and import it from Apple Health.

  1. Start a Surfing workout

    On the watch open Workout and pick Surfing; Water Sports works too. Apple Health has no wingfoil activity, so pick the closest one.

  2. Ride, then end the workout

    End it on the watch and let it save. The route is written to Health when the workout finishes, which can take a minute.

  3. Import it on the iPhone

    Import → Apple Health, allow CleanJibe to read workouts and routes — every switch on, or there is nothing to read — then pick yours.

  4. Keep it automatic

    Switch on Import new Health workouts automatically and the next session is waiting when you open the app.

  5. What you get

    Speed comes off the watch’s own receiver, so the records certify. Nothing records your wrist, so there are no pump strokes or takeoff attempts.

If you cannot wait for wind

twenty minutes, no water

Record a three to five minute walk on the watch. It proves the route from the watch to the phone and nothing else.

  1. Three to five minutes of movement

    A bike ride draws the nicest track — anything above about 12 km/h counts as flying. Walking works too. Go outside: indoors there are no positions.

  2. Bring it in by your own route

    Whichever route above matches your watch. It answers one question: does a recording made on your watch reach CleanJibe on your phone?

  3. What you should see

    A map with your track on it, duration and speeds at the top, a speed chart that moves when you drag along it.

  4. Turns and jibes reading zero

    Expected on a bike or on foot: there is nothing to jibe. This checks the route from the watch, never the analysis.

Then say how it read

the half only you can do

Menu → Support & ideas opens a mail with the facts filled in. TestFlight’s own feedback works too if you are on the beta.

  1. Read the verdicts against your memory

    Open the session, go to Turns: every turn says flew through, touched down or fell in, and the clean jibes are marked among them.

  2. Say where it disagrees with you

    A jibe you know you flew through marked as a touchdown, a swim it never noticed, a wind direction you did not sail in.

  3. Send it from the app

    Menu → Support & ideas fills in your build, phone, iOS version, watch and library size. You add what happened and press Send.

  4. Attach the file if a session read wrong

    With the .fit the detection can be re-run and fixed; without it there is only guesswork. Ideas and wishes are as welcome as faults.

If something does not work

Try the line that matches, then say so either way — a fix that took you three attempts is still worth hearing about.

“Nothing to import” from Apple Health
The workout is not in Health yet, or CleanJibe may not read it: iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → CleanJibe, everything on.
The watch says NO GPS for more than a minute
Open sky, wrist still, wait. A balcony under a roof is not open sky.
The session never reaches the iPhone
Open CleanJibe on the iPhone and leave it in front of you for a minute, Bluetooth on, the watch next to it.
CleanJibe is not offered in Apple’s Watch app
Reinstall CleanJibe on the iPhone, then look again under Available apps.
Garmin: CleanJibe is not in the activity list
Open Garmin Connect on the phone, let it sync, look again. The install can take a minute to reach the watch.
Garmin: nothing arrives from intervals.icu
Check the activity is visible on intervals.icu first — the Garmin link can take a few minutes — then sync again.
Garmin: the ZIP will not open in CleanJibe
Unzip it on the computer and AirDrop the .fit file inside instead.

What a good report says

One short message. A screenshot of the session page is worth more than a paragraph, and “this number looks wrong” is a perfectly good report.

Which route
Garmin, any .fit, Strava, the CleanJibe watch app, or Apple’s Workout app.
Your watch
Apple: model and watchOS version (Settings → General → About). Garmin: model and software version (Settings → System → About).
Did it arrive
Yes or no, and how long it took.
A screenshot
The session page. Was the track drawn? Were parts of it teal?
Anything odd
A permission you were never asked for, a “Nothing to import” screen, a NO GPS that never cleared, a crash, wording you did not understand.

Bonus: the share card and the video

Optional, two minutes, and the part we can least test on our own.

The share card

On the session page, tap the share button (top right). The share card is the picture with your track and your numbers on it. Send it to us the way you would send it to a friend, and say whether it looks good and whether anything on it is confusing.

Export video

In the same sheet: keep the 20 s preset, wait for the bar, then share the clip. Did the map draw itself smoothly? Did the end card look right? This one is new and has only ever been watched on a simulator.

What you should see

Open the session in CleanJibe on the iPhone. Three things say the route worked — after a session on the water you get the turn verdicts as well, which is the part only you can check.

A map with your track on it
Teal where you were “flying” (fast), grey where you were not.
Duration, distance and speeds at the top
Numbers that match roughly what you actually did.
A speed chart under the map
It moves when you drag along it.
Turns and jibes reading zero
Expected on a bike or on foot: there is nothing to jibe. You are testing the watch route here, not the wingfoil analysis.

Your old sessions

Once one test session has arrived, the interesting part is your real history. Everything CleanJibe imports is analysed as it lands: open a session and swipe through Ride, Turns, Takeoffs and Log, and Records and Trends fill up by themselves.

Apple Watch

+ → Import from Health lists every Surfing and Water Sports workout of the last two years. Tap Import all. Switch on the automatic pickup on that screen and new workouts arrive on their own from then on.

Recorded old sessions under another type — Sailing, say? Which workouts to offer, on the same screen, adds it.

Garmin

intervals.icu brings in what Garmin sends it from the day you connect the two, and Sync intervals.icu takes everything it finds there — so recent sessions come this way.

For the years before: in Garmin Connect on the web, Account → Export Your Data. Garmin mails you a ZIP; open it on the iPhone in CleanJibe (+ → Garmin export ZIP) and it imports every wingfoil and windsurf session inside at once. A few hundred sessions take a few minutes.

Strava

+ → Strava signs you in on Strava’s own consent page — read-only; CleanJibe writes nothing back — and lists your matching activities of the last two years, with the ones already in your library marked. Tap the ones you want, or Import all new. The one door that needs no Garmin account and no intervals.icu.

Strava hands over positions rather than the speed your watch measured, so those sessions’ speed records are marked uncertified. If the same afternoon is on intervals.icu, take it from there instead. And Strava lets a new app connect a limited number of riders, which the app says plainly; if you are told the app is full, tell us.

Anything else

+ → FIT, GPX, TCX or ZIP, or Open in CleanJibe from the iOS share sheet in any app that can export a workout — .fit, .gpx and .tcx all come in. That is the route for a Polar, a Suunto or a COROS, and the table on Which watch says what each format gives you.

Tell us what you saw

One short message per route. A screenshot of the session page is worth more than a paragraph, and “this number looks wrong” is a perfectly good report.

From inside the app — Menu → Support

The easiest door, and the one that arrives complete. Top left on the Sessions tab: Menu → Support. The same mail is under Settings → Send feedback, behind Something off? Send feedback at the foot of every page, and behind Report a problem with this session on a session’s share sheet — that last one names the session and attaches its card. Your build, your phone, your iOS version, your watch and the size of your library are already typed in; you add what happened and press Send. Nothing leaves the phone until you do.

Mail — the template is prefilled

info@cleanjibe.org — the link opens a mail with the fields already in it. Attach the screenshot and send.

TestFlight’s own feedback

In the TestFlight app: CleanJibe → Send Beta Feedback. Or take a screenshot anywhere inside CleanJibe and choose Share Beta Feedback. Either way it reaches us with your device, your iOS version and the exact build attached — nothing for you to look up.

Garmin, from the store listing

On the CleanJibe listing: Contact Developer for a message and Report a Problem for a bug, both of which land with the same person. A store review helps other riders find it at all.

What to put in it is the checklist above.

Something bigger than a test report — a feature, a number you disagree with, a session read wrong — is best as a GitHub issue, where the next rider with the same question can read the answer. Attach the .fit file if a session was read wrong: with the file the detection can be re-run and fixed, without it there is only guesswork.

Tell us. Bugs, verdicts you disagree with, and — just as much — ideas and wishes: what you would want this to do next. In the app, Menu → Support is the best door, because it prefills the facts; otherwise mail info@cleanjibe.org or open a GitHub issue. Need something that is only in the beta? Tell us.
