The routes, one per watch

Ride one session as you always do. Open it in CleanJibe. Check each turn: flew through, touchdown, fell in. Only you know which it really was.

Take the route that matches your wrist. Go outside: every route needs GPS, and a watch indoors records no positions. Every route ends the same way. The session lands in CleanJibe, analysed as it arrives.

Garmin with the CleanJibe watch app

fenix, epix, Forerunner and the rest

You get class A

Install it from Connect IQ, ride, save. Sessions sync through intervals.icu: Settings → intervals.icu, 4 steps, once. Show the 6 steps

  1. Install the watch app

    CleanJibe Wingfoil Tracker (Beta) is on the Connect IQ store. Take it from the web, or from Connect IQ inside the Garmin Connect app. No key, no account.

  2. Record and save

    Charge the watch and the phone. Press START, pick CleanJibe, wait for GPS good, press START again. Ride. Glance at the tally once. Then STOP and Save.

  3. Let Garmin Connect sync

    The watch uploads to Garmin Connect by itself next to the phone. Open Garmin Connect once if it is taking its time.

  4. Bridge it with intervals.icu

    Free account at intervals.icu, connect Garmin there, copy your personal API key, paste it into Settings → intervals.icu. 4 steps, about 5 minutes, once.

  5. Pull down on Sessions

    That is the refresh: CleanJibe checks intervals.icu, imports what is new and analyses it as it lands. Your session appears at the top.

  6. No intervals.icu? The long way

    connect.garmin.com → the activity → the gear icon → Export File. AirDrop it to the iPhone, then Import → FIT or ZIP….

The CleanJibe Apple Watch appbeta

Apple Watch · everything a Garmin gets

You get class B+

Record on your Apple Watch. The session comes to the phone by itself, with pump strokes and takeoff attempts included. Show the 4 steps

  1. Put it on the watch

    It comes with the CleanJibe beta. On the iPhone open Apple’s Watch app, scroll to Available apps, and install CleanJibe if it is missing.

  2. Record

    Open CleanJibe on the watch, allow Health and Location if it asks, wait until NO GPS clears, press START. Then Stop, and Done.

  3. Let it arrive

    Open CleanJibe on the iPhone. The session comes across on its own while phone and watch are together. Give it a minute.

  4. What you get

    Speed comes off the watch’s own receiver, so the records certify. The 50 Hz wrist gives pump strokes and takeoff attempts.

Apple’s own Workout appbeta

Apple Watch · nothing to install

You get class B

No extra app on the watch: record a Surfing workout and import it from Apple Health. Show the 5 steps

  1. Start a Surfing workout

    On the watch open Workout and pick Surfing. Water Sports works too. Apple Health has no wingfoil activity, so pick the closest one.

  2. Ride, then end the workout

    End it on the watch and let it save. The route is written to Health when the workout finishes, which can take a minute.

  3. Import it on the iPhone

    Import → Apple Health. Allow CleanJibe to read workouts and routes. Switch every one on, or there is nothing to read. Then pick yours.

  4. Keep it automatic

    Switch on Import new Health workouts automatically and the next session is waiting when you open the app.

  5. What you get

    Speed comes off the watch’s own receiver, so the records certify. Nothing records your wrist, so there are no pump strokes or takeoff attempts.

Any watch that writes a .fit

Polar, Suunto, COROS and everything else

You get class B

Sync it through intervals.icu, or share the file into CleanJibe from Files, Mail, AirDrop or any share sheet. Show the 5 steps

  1. Record on your own watch

    Any outdoor activity with GPS, recorded the way you always do. There is nothing of ours to install on these watches.

  2. Export the workout as a file

    Suunto: Calendar → the workout → ⋯ → FIT. COROS: Activities → the activity → ⋯ → Export → FIT. Polar exports from flow.polar.com only.

  3. Take the FIT where you are offered one

    A FIT carries the speed your watch measured. A .gpx does not. A .tcx carries it only when the app wrote it in.

  4. Hand it to CleanJibe

    Open in CleanJibe from the iOS share sheet, or Import → FIT or ZIP…. Foil time, flights, turns and the map are identical either way.

  5. Or let intervals.icu do it

    Most of these watches sync to intervals.icu too. CleanJibe picks them up from there, through the same 4 steps as the Garmin route.

Strava

any watch that syncs to Strava

You get class C

Settings → Strava → Connect with Strava, then Import → Import from Strava…. Positions only, records uncertified. Without the watch’s own speed, a fall can read as a touchdown. Show the 5 steps

  1. Connect once

    Settings → Strava → Connect with Strava. Strava’s own consent page opens. Leave the private-activities box ticked, or your “Only you” sessions stay hidden.

  2. Pick your sessions

    Import → Import from Strava… lists your matching activities of the last two years. Tap the ones you want, or Import all new.

  3. Keep it automatic

    Once one session has come in this way a toggle appears, and CleanJibe checks Strava whenever you open the app.

  4. What it costs

    Strava hands over positions rather than the speed your watch measured. These speed records are marked uncertified. There are no pump strokes.

  5. If connecting is refused

    Strava lets a new app connect a limited number of riders. That is nothing to do with your account. Menu → Support & ideas says so.

If you cannot wait for wind

20 minutes, no water
Record a 3 to 5 minute walk on the watch. It proves the route from the watch to the phone, nothing else. Show the 4 steps

  1. 3 to 5 minutes of movement

    A bike ride draws the nicest track. Anything above about 12 km/h counts as flying. Walking works too. Go outside: indoors there are no positions.

  2. Bring it in by your own route

    Whichever route above matches your watch. It answers one question: does a recording made on your watch reach CleanJibe on your phone?

  3. What you should see

    A map shows your track, with duration and speeds at the top. A speed chart moves when you drag along it.

  4. Turns and jibes reading zero

    Expected on a bike or on foot: there is nothing to jibe. This checks the route from the watch, never the analysis.

Then say how it read

your verdicts against the engine’s
Menu → Support & ideas opens a mail with the facts filled in. TestFlight’s own feedback works too if you are on the beta. Show the 5 steps

  1. Read the verdicts against your memory

    Open the session and go to Turns. Every turn says flew through, touched down or fell in. The clean jibes are marked among them.

  2. Say where it disagrees with you

    A jibe you know you flew through, marked as a touchdown. A swim it never noticed, or a wind direction you did not sail in.

  3. Send it from the app

    Menu → Support & ideas fills in your build, phone, iOS version, watch and library size. You add what happened and press Send.

  4. Attach the file if a session read wrong

    With the .fit the detection can be re-run and fixed. Without it there is only guesswork. Ideas and wishes are as welcome as faults.

  5. Send a card, and say how it reads

    The share button on the session page draws a share card with your track and your numbers. Send it to a friend. Say if anything on it reads wrong.

If something does not work

Try the line that matches. Then say how it went, either way. A fix that took you 3 attempts is worth hearing about.
The watch app does not start
Restart the watch once. If it still does not start, send us the message it shows.
“Nothing to import” from Apple Health
The workout is not in Health yet, or CleanJibe may not read it: iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → CleanJibe, everything on.
The watch says NO GPS for more than a minute
Open sky, wrist still, wait. A balcony under a roof is not open sky.
The session never reaches the iPhone
Open CleanJibe on the iPhone and leave it in front of you for a minute, Bluetooth on, the watch next to it.
CleanJibe is not offered in Apple’s Watch app
Reinstall CleanJibe on the iPhone, then look again under Available apps.
Garmin: CleanJibe is not in the activity list
Open Garmin Connect on the phone, let it sync, look again. The install can take a minute to reach the watch.
Garmin: nothing arrives from intervals.icu
Check the activity is visible on intervals.icu first. The Garmin link can take a few minutes. Then sync again.
Garmin: the ZIP will not open in CleanJibe
Unzip it on the computer and AirDrop the .fit file inside instead.

What a good report says

One short message. A screenshot of the session page beats a paragraph. “This number looks wrong” is a good report.

Which route
Garmin, any .fit, Strava, the CleanJibe watch app, or Apple’s Workout app.
Your watch
Apple: model and watchOS version, under Settings → General → About. Garmin: model and software version, under Settings → System → About.
Did it arrive
Yes or no, and how long it took.
A screenshot
The session page. Was the track drawn? Were parts of it teal?
Anything odd
A permission you were never asked for, a “Nothing to import” screen, a NO GPS that never cleared, a crash, wording you did not understand.

On Android

There is no CleanJibe app for Android and none is needed. Open the browser app and drop the file in. It keeps a library, records and trends like the iPhone app.

Install it, and it joins the share sheet. In Chrome, take Add to Home screen from the menu. It may be called Install app. After that, hold a .fit or .gpx anywhere on the phone and choose Share → CleanJibe. The analysis opens on it, with the file still on the phone.

Your old sessions

Once one session has arrived, the interesting part is your back catalogue. Everything CleanJibe imports is analysed as it lands, so Records and Trends fill up by themselves. One button, +, holds every door. Import from Health takes an Apple Watch. Sync intervals.icu and the Garmin export ZIP take a Garmin. The ZIP carries your whole history in one import. Strava takes anything that reaches it. FIT, GPX, TCX or ZIP takes a Polar, a Suunto, a COROS or any file at all.

Strava hands over positions, not the speed your watch measured. Those sessions’ speed records are marked uncertified. Take the same afternoon off intervals.icu instead where you can. Strava lets a new app connect a limited number of riders, and the app says so plainly. If you are told the app is full, tell us.

Not bridged to intervals.icu yet? The four steps, once
  1. Create a free intervals.icu account
  2. Connect Garmin in intervals.icu
  3. Generate your personal API key
  4. Paste the key into CleanJibe

Step four ends on the app's own button, Save & check. It verifies the key and says how many activities it can see. The iPhone asks for one thing, your personal API key. The Athlete ID is the browser analyzer's.

Tell us what you saw

Three doors, all to the same person. What goes in the message is the checklist above.

Three doors, cheapest first

  • Menu → Support & ideas, top left on the Sessions tab. It fills in your build, your phone, the paired watch and how big your library is. You add what happened and press Send. Nothing leaves the phone until you do.
  • A GitHub issue for anything bigger. It stays readable by the next rider with the same question.
  • info@cleanjibe.org, which opens with the template already in the body. Attach the .fit if a session read wrong. With the file the detection can be re-run and fixed. Without it there is only guesswork.

Both stores have a report button of their own, and both reach the same person: Get the beta names them.
