Did you fly through that jibe?

CleanJibe reads a wingfoil session off your watch and tells you what actually happened: how much of it you spent on the foil, how long each flight lasted, your speed records, and — for every turn — whether you flew through it, touched down, or fell in.

Get the beta Try the analyzer Free. No account. The analyzer runs in your browser — the file is never uploaded, and there is an example session inside if you have no file to hand.

Installed? Start here: your first session on the water — charge, record, save, and what you should see on the phone afterwards.

The three pieces

The watch records and shows the live numbers; the phone and the browser do the full analysis afterwards. All three run the same analysis code, so a session means the same thing wherever you read it. Use any one of them on its own.

What you need, what you get

Everything reads a file, so what you get back depends on what the recording carries. Three classes, and every session lands in one of them.

Class You record with You get You do not get
Class A · Garmin watch app The CleanJibe watch app on a Garmin Everything: foil time, flights, every turn verdict and clean jibe, certified speed records, the wind axis, pump strokes and takeoff attempts Nothing missing
Class B · any speed-certified file Any other FIT with a measured speed channel — Garmin’s own Windsurf profile, another Connect IQ app Foil time, flights, turn verdicts and clean jibes, certified speed records, the wind axis Pump strokes and takeoff attempts — shown as absent, never as zero
Class B+ · Apple Watch appbeta The CleanJibe Apple Watch app: class b speed, plus the 50 Hz wrist accelerometer Everything class b gets, and pump strokes and takeoff attempts, analysed on the phone Nothing missing; the watch itself detects nothing live
Class C · positions only Positions only: Strava, a .gpx, a .tcx without speed, a phone in a pocket Foil time, flights, turn verdicts and clean jibes, the wind axis, and speed records estimated from positions and marked uncertified Certified records, pump strokes, takeoff attempts

beta in the public beta today, not yet in the App Store release.

Which class a watch lands in, brand by brand, is on Which watch — including the phone-only route, which is class c and works. Take the FIT wherever an app offers you one: it carries the speed your watch measured, which is the whole difference between b and c.

Curious about what is coming

The App Store release is the steady half of the app. Everything below is real and runs today — the difference is who can get at it.

In the beta todaybeta

Install the beta and you have all of it, this afternoon — there is no waiting list and no second install:

  • GPX and TCX imports, so a Polar, a Suunto or a COROS comes straight in.
  • The Garmin export ZIP: your whole history in one import.
  • Apple Health both ways — read your Workout recordings, write your sessions back as workouts.
  • The CleanJibe Apple Watch recorder, recording straight to the phone.
  • Home-screen widgets: the last session, and this week.
  • Session video export — the map draws itself along your track.
  • Grouping and filters in the library, for when there are a hundred sessions.
