Where this lives

    

      Saved sessions are written to this browser's private per-origin storage
      (OPFS, or IndexedDB where OPFS is unavailable). They are not synced, not backed up and
      not visible to any other site. Clearing this site's data, or using a private
      window, deletes them. Use Download all, or the per-row
      .fit / .json buttons, to keep a copy you own.
