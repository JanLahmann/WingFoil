Did you fly through that jibe?

CleanJibe reads a wingfoil session off your watch and tells you what actually happened: how much of it you spent on the foil, how long each flight lasted, your speed records, and — for every turn — whether you flew through it, touched down, or fell in.

Start with the browser analyzer. Drop in a .fit file from any Garmin watch — the CleanJibe app, or Garmin's own Windsurf profile — and you get the whole session back in a few seconds. There is also a watch app that shows the same numbers live on your wrist, and an iPhone app that keeps every session. Each one works on its own.

The three pieces

The watch records and shows the live numbers; the phone and the browser do the full analysis afterwards. All three run the same analysis code, so a session means the same thing wherever you read it. Use any one of them on its own.

What you need

No watch app? Still works

The analyzer reads any .fit file. A session recorded with Garmin's own Windsurf profile, or with another wingfoil Connect IQ app, gives you the track, the flights, the jibe outcomes, the speed records and the wind axis. Only pump strokes and takeoff effort need the CleanJibe watch app, because only it records the wrist accelerometer.

Android

There is no Android app, and the analyzer does not need one — open cleanjibe.org/app in your phone browser and it works the same, library and trends included. They are stored in that browser, on that phone.

Another watch brand

The engine reads standard .fit files, but every file it has been tested against came off a Garmin. If your watch exports .fit, try it — it costs one drag and nothing is uploaded. .gpx works too, from any watch or phone app: the track, the flights and the turn verdicts all come out, but its speed records are estimated from positions rather than measured, so they are marked uncertified, and there is no pump data.

What it counts

The same handful of numbers on the wrist, on the phone and on this site — so a session means the same thing wherever you read it.

on the foil flew through touched down fell in

Foil %
The share of the session actually flying, rather than merely moving.
Flights & touchdowns
One takeoff starts a flight; a touchdown or a swim ends it. Both are counted.
Jibe outcomes
Every turn gets a verdict: flew through, touched down, fell in. A clean jibe is one you fly all the way through, carrying your speed.
Dry streaks
How many jibes in a row you stayed out of the water — and the best run of the day.
JPH
Dry jibes per hour. Falling in more often cannot raise it.
WPH
Swims per hour. The number nobody wants, kept honest anyway.
Speed records
GP3S-style windows: best 2 s, best 1 NM, alpha 500, best hour.
Pump & takeoff effort
Strokes counted off the accelerometer, and which attempts got you flying.

How it is built

Your file stays yours

The analyzer has no server to upload to. Your recording is read, analyzed and drawn inside your own browser tab: no account, no upload, nothing to sign up for. These pages count visits with a cookie-free page counter that never sees your session; nothing about your ride goes anywhere.

The thresholds are published

What counts as a flight, a touchdown or a fallen jibe is written down in the public repository rather than hidden in a product, so you can check the numbers against what you remember and argue with the ones that are wrong.
