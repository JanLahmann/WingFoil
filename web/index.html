What CleanJibe measures

On foil
How much of your session you spent flying rather than just moving.
Flights & touchdowns
One takeoff starts a flight. A touchdown or a fall ends it. Both are counted.
Dry streak
How many jibes in a row you stayed out of the water, and the best run of the day.
Speed records
Your fastest 2 seconds, 10 seconds, 500 m and nautical mile. The speedsurfing world uses the same windows.

Every turn, judged

Flew through
The turn kept the foil, from the sweep until you were flying again.
Touchdown
The foil went in and you kept going. Dry, and not clean.
Fell in
Every time you ended up in the water, in a turn or in a straight line.
Clean
A jibe that flew through, held 70 % of its entry speed, and stayed quiet for 10 s.

CleanJibe is a family of apps on one analysis engine.

A Garmin and an iPhone

You get everything. Add the watch app for a verdict on your wrist after every jibe.

  • iPhone app It reads every session from Garmin, Strava and other watches, judges every turn and keeps your library.
  • Garmin watch app It records on your wrist, with live numbers and a summary when you save.

Bring in the sessions you already have

Your last season is already on your watch account. Connect it once and CleanJibe reads every session you rode.

Any Garmin profile
Windsurf, SUP, Kitesurf or Surfing all count. So does any activity you named wing or foil.
Through intervals.icu
Garmin has no open API, so the free intervals.icu is the bridge. Two years of sessions come across.
Or one file at a time
Drop a .fit, .gpx or .tcx into the browser app. It is read on your device and never uploaded.
