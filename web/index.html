Every jibe, judged.

Did you fly through that jibe? CleanJibe reads your session off the watch. It tells you your time on the foil, every flight, your speed records, and a verdict on every turn. Flew through, touchdown, or fell in.

Get the beta Open the app See a real session Free. No account. It runs in your browser. The file is never uploaded.

The three pieces

The watch records and shows the live numbers. The phone and the browser do the full analysis afterwards. All three run the same analysis code, so a session means the same thing wherever you read it.
