What your session actually did.

CleanJibe is an open tracking system for wing foiling. A Garmin watch app records the flights, the jibes and the swims while they happen; an iPhone app and this site take the recording apart afterwards. All three run the same analysis, and the rules it applies are published rather than guessed at.

A clean jibe is one you fly all the way through, without touching down and without swimming. That is the verdict this project puts on every turn you make — so the name is a number, not a slogan.

The three pieces

CleanJibe is the home of the WingFoil apps: the watch does the recording and the live numbers, the phone and the browser do the deep analysis. You can use any one of them on its own.

What it counts

The same handful of numbers on the wrist, on the phone and on this site — so a session means the same thing wherever you read it.

Foil %
The share of the session actually flying, rather than merely moving.
Flights & touchdowns
One takeoff starts a flight; a touchdown or a swim ends it. Both are counted.
Jibe outcomes
Every turn gets a verdict: flew through, touched down, fell in.
Dry streaks
How many jibes in a row you stayed out of the water — and the best run of the day.
JPH
Dry jibes per hour. Falling in more often cannot raise it.
WPH
Swims per hour. The number nobody wants, kept honest anyway.
Speed records
GP3S-style windows: best 2 s, best 1 NM, alpha 500, best hour.
Pump & takeoff effort
Strokes counted off the accelerometer, and which attempts got you flying.

How it is built

One engine, everywhere

The phone and this site run the same analysis code — the browser literally runs the project's Python, compiled to WebAssembly. A number that differs between them is a bug, and there are tests that say so.

Thresholds in the open

What counts as a flight, a touchdown or a fallen jibe is written down in the repository, not hidden in a product. The values are tunable, and the reasoning behind them is there to argue with.

Your file stays yours

The analyzer has no backend to upload to. Your recording is read, analyzed and drawn inside your own browser tab: no account, no upload, no tracking.
