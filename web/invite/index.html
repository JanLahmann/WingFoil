Open beta · free · nothing to request

Get the beta

Both CleanJibe apps are free, open betas. The watch app installs from the Connect IQ store; the iPhone app installs from a public TestFlight link. There is no invite to apply for, no code to send in, and nobody to wait on — and the beta carries everything on this list today.

Installed, and now what? Get started: your first session on the water — charge, record, save, and read the verdicts against what you remember. There is a 20-minute dry run in it too, for a week with no wind.

Not sure you want either yet? The browser analyzer is not a beta at all — it reads a .fit file from any Garmin watch and gives you the whole session back. There is an example inside if you have no file to hand.

The Garmin watch app

Two steps, and neither of them is a mail to a person.

  1. Install it from the Connect IQ store

    CleanJibe Wingfoil Tracker (Beta) on apps.garmin.com — install it from the web store or from Connect IQ on your phone, the same way as any other watch app.

    Watches, as of 0.9.10 — 39 Connect IQ products: the fenix 8 family (43 mm, 47 mm, Solar 47/51 mm, Pro 47 mm), the fenix 7 family (7 / 7S / 7X and every Pro variant), epix 2 and epix 2 Pro, Forerunner 255 / 265 / 955 / 965 / 570 / 970, MARQ 2, Enduro 3, D2 Mach 1 and 2, Descent Mk3 43 mm — Venu 2 / 2S / 2 Plus / 3 / 3S, vívoactive 5 / 6 and Instinct 3 AMOLED 45 / 50 mm since 0.9.10 — and, new in 0.9.11, the fenix 5 Plus family (5 Plus / 5S Plus / 5X Plus). Those last four families have passed the layout checks in the simulator and have never been ridden on a real watch; if yours is one of them, tell us how the pages look and whether the touch controls behave. Full list, family by family, on Which watch. If the store does not offer your watch, the install button will say so.

  2. Ride

    Open CleanJibe on the watch and start. It records as a Windsurf activity — so it lands in Garmin Connect as what it is rather than as a walk — and it works out the wind direction for itself after a few minutes of riding.

The iPhone app

TestFlight, on a public link. Open it on the phone and you are in.

  1. Open the TestFlight link

    testflight.apple.com/join/nygqGGcn — on the iPhone itself. No address to submit and no invite mail to wait for.

  2. Install and open

    You need Apple's TestFlight app from the App Store if you do not have it; the link installs CleanJibe through it. TestFlight builds expire, so it will ask you to update every so often.

  3. Open the example first

    The app ships with a real session already in it, so you can see the whole thing — map, turn list, replay, records — before importing anything of your own.

    Then bring in your own. Import has four doors: the Garmin history ZIP, single files, Apple Health, and Strava — which signs you in on Strava's own consent page, reads only, and needs no Garmin account at all. Files can be .fit, .gpx or .tcx, and they do not have to be picked: Open in CleanJibe appears in the iOS share sheet wherever one of the three is offered, which is how a Polar, Suunto or COROS session gets in straight from the vendor's own app. Take the FIT where an app offers one — it carries the speed the watch measured; a GPX does not, and a TCX does only when it has a speed channel.

The iPhone app does not need the watch app. It reads Garmin's own Windsurf recordings too; only pump strokes and takeoff effort need CleanJibe on the wrist, because only it records the wrist accelerometer. Which brands get what is one table, on Which watch; what has changed in each build is on What’s new.

It’s a beta — tell us what’s wrong and what’s missing

Every report lands with the one developer. A rough sentence beats a polished bug report you never send, and “this number looks wrong” is a perfectly good one.

From inside the app: Menu → Support

The best door, because it fills in the facts for you. Top left on the Sessions tab: Menu → Support. The same mail is under Settings → Send feedback, behind Something off? Send feedback at the foot of every page, and behind Report a problem with this session on a session's share sheet — that last one names the session and attaches its card. The build, your phone, your iOS version, the paired watch and how big your library is are already in it; you write what happened and press Send. Nothing leaves the phone until you do.

A GitHub issue — for anything bigger

github.com/JanLahmann/WingFoil/issues takes a screenshot or a .fit file, holds a thread, and stays visible to the next rider who hits the same thing instead of disappearing into one inbox. Use it for a feature, a number you disagree with, or a question whose answer other people want too.

Or plain mail

info@cleanjibe.org — no account, no app needed, and the right door when the app is the thing that will not start. What you rode, what it said, and what you expected it to say. If a session was read wrong, attach the .fit file: with the file the detection can be re-run and fixed, without it there is only guesswork.

Tell us. Bugs, verdicts you disagree with, and — just as much — what you would want this to do next. Ideas and wishes are as welcome as bugs. In the app, Menu → Support is the best door, because it prefills the facts; otherwise mail info@cleanjibe.org or open a GitHub issue. Need something that is only in the beta? Tell us.

What is most useful: turns scored wrong, flights the watch missed or invented, a wind direction that disagrees with the day, and anything that stopped, froze or drained the battery. Which watch and which app version, if you have them to hand — and if you came in through Menu → Support, you already have.

The stores’ own channels reach us too: in TestFlight, take a screenshot inside the app and send it — the built-in feedback form arrives here with the build and the device attached; on the Connect IQ store, Contact Developer on either listing does the same. Handy on the beach; an issue is still easier to answer.

Coming in a future release

The App Store release, when it comes, will be the steady half of this app. The beta is ahead of it — and a third build, dev, is ahead of the beta and is not public.

In the beta today

The TestFlight link at the top of this page hands over all of it this afternoon. Nothing here is a promise; it is what installs:

  • .gpx and .tcx files, so a session exported from a Polar, a Suunto or a COROS opens straight from Files.
  • Apple Health, both ways: what Apple's Workout app recorded is read in, and your sessions are written back as workouts.
  • The CleanJibe Apple Watch app, which records on your wrist with live numbers and hands the session to the phone.
  • Home-screen widgets and the watch complication.
  • The session video: your afternoon as a film rather than a card.
  • Grouping the library by month, year or spot, and filtering it.

The Garmin export ZIP — your whole history in one import — is in every build, beta or not.

If neither is for you

The analyzer needs nothing

cleanjibe.org/app is free, has no account and is not a beta. Drop in a .fit file from any Garmin watch and you get the session back: foil time, every flight, the verdict on every turn, and your speed records. The file never leaves the browser tab.

Android is fine

The analyzer is a web page, so it runs in a phone browser as well as on a desktop — library and trends included. There is no Android app because there is nothing for one to do that the page does not.

Questions before you install

Ask in an issue — or mail info@cleanjibe.org if you would rather not. The code and the detection thresholds are readable first, either way: github.com/JanLahmann/WingFoil.
