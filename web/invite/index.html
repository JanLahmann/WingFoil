Open beta · free · nothing to request

Get the beta

Both CleanJibe apps are free, open betas. No invite, no code, nobody to wait on.

The watch app installs from the Connect IQ store. The iPhone app installs from a public TestFlight link. The beta carries everything on this list today.

Installed, and now what? Get started: your first session on the water. Record, save, and read the verdicts against what you remember.

The Garmin watch app

Two steps, and neither of them is a mail to a person.

  1. Install it from the Connect IQ store

    CleanJibe Wingfoil Tracker (Beta) on apps.garmin.com. Install it from the web store, or from Connect IQ on your phone. It works the same way as any other watch app.

    42 watches at 0.9.13. If the store does not offer yours, the install button says so. Which watch says what each class of recording gets you.

  2. Ride

    Open CleanJibe on the watch and start. It records as a Windsurf activity. It lands in Garmin Connect as what it is, not as a walk. It works out the wind direction for itself after a few minutes.

The iPhone app

TestFlight, on a public link. Open it on the phone and you are in.

  1. Open the TestFlight link

    testflight.apple.com/join/nygqGGcn, on the iPhone itself. No address to submit and no invite mail to wait for.

  2. Install and open

    You need Apple's TestFlight app if you do not have it. The link installs CleanJibe through it. TestFlight builds expire, so it will ask you to update now and then.

  3. Open the example first

    The app ships with a real session already in it. See the whole thing before importing anything of your own. Map, turn list, replay, records.

    Then bring in your own: Getting started has the route for your watch, step by step.

beta The beta iPhone app brings the CleanJibe Apple Watch app with it. Install it from the iPhone’s Watch app. It records on the wrist and hands the session over.

The iPhone app does not need the watch app. Which brands get what is one table, on Which watch. What each build changed is below.

What’s new

Newest first. The iPhone app ships as TestFlight builds. The watch app ships as Connect IQ versions. The two number differently and land on different days.

TestFlight offers the newest build by itself. Garmin Connect updates the watch app. A report is worth more with a build number in it. You find yours at the foot of the app’s menu, and under Settings → About.

It’s a beta. Tell us what’s wrong and what’s missing

Every report lands with the one developer. A rough sentence beats a polished bug report you never send. Most useful: a turn scored wrong, or anything that stopped, froze or drained the battery.

From inside the app: Menu → Support & ideas

The best door, because it fills the facts in for you. The build, your phone, the paired watch and your library size are already in the mail. Find it top left on the Sessions tab.

A session’s share sheet has it too, as Report a problem with this session…. That one attaches the session’s card.

A GitHub issue, for anything bigger

github.com/JanLahmann/WingFoil/issues takes a screenshot or a .fit file. It holds a thread, and stays readable by the next rider who hits the same thing.

Or plain mail

info@cleanjibe.org needs no account and no app. It is the right door when the app will not start. If a session was read wrong, attach the .fit file. The detection can then be re-run and fixed.

The stores’ own channels reach us too. In TestFlight, Send Beta Feedback arrives here with the build and the device attached. So does a screenshot taken inside CleanJibe and sent with Share Beta Feedback.

On the Connect IQ listing, Contact Developer and Report a Problem do the same. Handy on the beach. An issue is still easier to answer.

Coming in a future release

The App Store release, when it comes, will be the steady half. The beta is ahead of it. A third build, dev, is ahead of the beta and is not public.

In the beta today

The TestFlight link above hands over all of it this afternoon. Nothing here is a promise. This is what installs:

  • .gpx and .tcx files, so a session exported from a Polar, a Suunto or a COROS opens straight from Files.
  • Apple Health, both ways: what Apple's Workout app recorded is read in, and your sessions are written back as workouts.
  • The CleanJibe Apple Watch app, which records on your wrist with live numbers and hands the session to the phone.
  • Home-screen widgets and the watch complication.
  • The session video: your afternoon as a film rather than a card.
  • Grouping the library by month, year or spot, and filtering it.
  • Planned: a live view on the Apple Watch. Foil state, flights, records and turn verdicts on the wrist, as the Garmin app shows them.

The Garmin export ZIP is in every build, beta or not. It brings your whole history in one import.
